Instructions

Start with €100 in virtual funds. Choose one of five horses and enter your stake. Watch the race unfold: every horse has an equal chance to win. A winning selection returns five times your stake, including the original stake. A losing selection costs your stake. Keep playing until you run out of virtual funds, or return to the menu. Progress is saved automatically after each completed race.

No real money, purchases, prizes or cash-out. Inspired by the 1983 ZX Spectrum game published in The Spectrum Book of Games.
